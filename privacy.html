FlashyCards Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

FlashyCards is a flashcard app that works on your iPhone without an account. We don't collect your personal data. Your decks, cards, and study history stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Information we collect

We don't collect, receive, or sell any information about you. FlashyCards has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone so that it can work:

  • Decks and cards that you create or import, including their text, images, and audio
  • Your study history, scheduling data, statistics, and settings

We can't access this information. Your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include it, depending on your device settings.

Permissions

FlashyCards only asks for these permissions when you use the related feature:

  • Camera: to take a photo for a card
  • Photos: to add a photo you choose to a card
  • Microphone: to record audio for a card

Photos and recordings you add are saved in your cards on your device. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

When information leaves your device

  • Backups you share: a backup file contains all of your FlashyCards data. It goes only where you send it from the iOS share sheet, such as Files.
  • Online content in imported decks: a deck you import may include images or other content hosted on the web. Showing that content can connect your device to the website hosting it. That website may receive technical information such as your IP address. FlashyCards doesn't control these websites.
  • Links in cards: a web link you tap in a card opens in Safari, where the website's own privacy policy applies.
  • Apple: if you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymized crash reports and usage data. Apple's handling of this data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual cards and decks in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, but doesn't remove backup files you've saved elsewhere or your device's own backups.

Because we don't hold your data, we have nothing to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

Children

FlashyCards doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, and has no features for sharing content with other people.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, we'll update this policy first.
