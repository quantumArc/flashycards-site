Common questions

How do I add cards?

Open the Create tab, choose a deck or tap + to make a new one, then fill in the front and back of the card.

What files can I import?

Open the Import tab to bring in JSON, CSV, or .apkg deck files.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You don't need an account, and studying works offline. Your data stays on your device. A card from an imported deck may need a connection if it uses content hosted online.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?

Open the Import tab and tap Back Up All Data, then save the file somewhere safe, such as Files. On the other iPhone, tap Restore from Backup and choose that file.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app, and this can't be undone.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete a deck from the Decks tab. To delete a single card, find it in the Search tab and open it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.
